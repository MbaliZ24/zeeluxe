How booking works

Three steps. No account, no waiting on a website.

1. Fill in the form

Choose your service, date and time, and describe the look or style you want.

2. Send it on WhatsApp

Your booking opens in WhatsApp, ready to send to Zeeluxe on 061 985 0213.

3. Pay your deposit

A 50% deposit secures your date. You'll get a confirmation once it's paid.

Book your appointment

Tap the button when you're done. WhatsApp opens with your booking already written. Press send to complete it.

Bookings before 8am add R100. Housecalls need two days' notice.

Policy & terms

By confirming your booking, you agree to the terms below.

Deposit

A 50% deposit is required to secure your booking. Deposits are non-refundable. If you cancel, your deposit is kept as a cancellation fee.

Early appointments

A R100 fee applies to appointments before 8am.

Arriving on time

Please arrive on time. A 15 minute grace period is allowed. If you are later and have not communicated, your appointment will be cancelled.

Housecalls

Book at least two days in advance. The callout is R300 and may change depending on where you live.

Your visit

Please avoid coming with infants or toddlers. You may bring one person with you, no more.
